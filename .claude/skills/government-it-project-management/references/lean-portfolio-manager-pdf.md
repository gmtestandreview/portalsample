These knowledge check questions are designed to be used at the end of each SAFe course lesson to check for learning and
comprehension. They are optional and can be shared with the class in a variety of formats including live class discussion,
small group, discussion, or for individual student reflection. The questions can also be copied/pasted into a polling tool for
real-time class feedback and discussion.


## Lesson 1:

1.What are the three essential collaborations necessary for Lean portfolio management implementation? (Choose
three.)

A.  Program increment planning
B.  Lean governance
C.  Agile portfolio operations
D.  Participatory budgeting
E.  Strategy and investment funding
F.  Agile program management office

Correct Answer: B,C,E

2.Which objective is achieved by organizing a portfolio around value?

A.  Faster learning and shorter time to market
B.  True cost accounting metrics
C.  Increased task completion
D.  Communication across the organization

## Correct Answer: A
