## Migration Notes

- `forms`, `Inputs`, `modals`, `Header`, `Footer`, and `Layout` are the highest
  value component families because they define most route-level composition.
- `Utilities` and `RouteLeavingGuard` should remain documented even when they are
  not visually rich, because they affect behaviour during migration.
- `RequestList`, `SearchFilter`, `Pagination`, and `Pill` encode dashboard state
  semantics that should not be re-invented during a design-system rewrite.
- Type Approval migration also depends on `Inputs/Attachment`,
  `Inputs/CertificateNumberLookup`, `Inputs/CheckboxGroup`, `Progress`,
  `RequestList/paRequestItem`, `SearchFilter/TypeApproval`, and `SlateEditor`.
