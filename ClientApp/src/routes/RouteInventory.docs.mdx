## Migration Notes

- Routes with `displayHeaderAndFooter={false}` are multi-step form surfaces and
  should migrate with wizard-shell parity first.
- `AuthenticatedElement` is the dominant route wrapper and remains a migration
  dependency even when route components are moved incrementally.
- Error routes are not decorative pages; they encode real HTTP status behaviour
  and must stay visible in Storybook during migration.
